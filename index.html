Using the Ottawa Historical Aerial Photo Finder

Explore historic aerial photographs of Ottawa and Gatineau by survey year and flight. Each blue dot marks the approximate centre of a photograph. When you click a dot, a dashed orange circle gives a rough coverage guide derived from the listed photo scale, assuming a standard 9-inch aerial negative. It is not a surveyed image boundary; original film dimensions, camera geometry, tilt, orientation, cropping, and terrain may differ. Do not use the circle to rule a location in or out of a photograph.

Find a photograph

  1. Choose a Survey year (or All years).
  2. Leave Flight on All flights, or select a specific flight.
  3. Click a blue dot to see its photo ID and details; choose Preview to view the scan, or open the original image in a new tab.
  4. Use Find photo ID if you already know an identifier such as A2199_49.

Map background: Choose Esri streets, Esri topographic, or satellite imagery. Switching backgrounds preserves your map position, zoom, photo pins, and selected flight. Satellite imagery is modern, not a historical aerial survey. Your choice is remembered when browser storage is available.

Flight highlighting: Select a flight to show its photo centres in amber, without connecting lines. With Show neighbouring flights checked, other flights from the chosen year remain as blue dots so you can see overlaps. Uncheck it to show only the selected flight. Use Zoom to flight to fit its indexed photo centres on the map. When All years is selected, nearby dots can include other years.

Matched digitized images only: When checked, the map shows locations linked to university image filenames. Uncheck it to see additional locations from the geographic index; some may not have online images.

Show approximate coverage on pin click: Toggle the orange dashed guide on or off. It is displayed for one selected photo at a time to keep the map readable. The estimated circle diameter is about 0.229 metres multiplied by the photo scale denominator (for example, about 2.3 km at 1:10,000). The circle is only a visual aid, not a verified footprint.

Photo loading: Only the selected photograph is requested. Previously viewed photographs may load faster from your browser cache. The image is displayed directly as the browser receives it; a small status message appears if loading takes longer than a moment.

Slow or failed images: If a photograph takes more than 15 seconds, the viewer explains that it is still loading. A failed download offers a Retry photograph button; the original full-image link remains available.

Browse a flight: In a photograph preview, use Previous / Next or the left/right arrow keys to move through the available digitized photographs in that flight. Press Escape to close the preview. A status message appears if a new scan takes more than a moment; the sequence follows photograph numbers and skips missing images.

Year counts: Shows how many indexed and matched locations are available by year.

For people adding photographs

Add university directory listing matches copied JPEG filenames to indexed photo locations. Export / Import directory matches backs up those additions. Export / Import discoveries separately backs up links verified through the preview feature. These advanced controls are optional for ordinary browsing.

Sources and limitations

Photograph scans are hosted by the University of Ottawa aerial photograph archive. Photo positions and metadata come from the National Air Photo Library geographic index. Map backgrounds use Esri or OpenStreetMap tiles, depending on your selection. This is an independent historical research viewer, not an official University of Ottawa or National Air Photo Library service.

Photo locations and coverage guides are approximate, image availability can change, and large scans may take time to load. Neighbouring photographs are not preloaded, to avoid competing downloads. An internet connection is required for the map background and online photographs.
